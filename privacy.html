Join the Points! — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Join the Points! (package com.msalvide.jointhepoints) handles, developed by Matias Salvide ("I", "me"). Contact: msalvidecuenta@gmail.com.

Summary

  • The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. I do not collect or receive your personal information.
  • Your progress stays on your device.
  • The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which collects some device information as described below.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.

1. Data stored on your device

The game saves the following only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is never sent to me or to anyone else:

  • the board in progress and your settings (colour palette, language, zoom);
  • the gallery of boards you chose to save;
  • whether the full version is unlocked.

You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the game.

2. Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK collects and processes, under Google's own responsibility:

  • the device's advertising ID;
  • the IP address, used to estimate an approximate location (country or city);
  • device information (model, operating system version, language);
  • interactions with ads (views, taps) and diagnostic data (crashes, performance).

This data is sent over encrypted connections to Google. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice later from the game's menu. Without consent, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use the data above for limited purposes such as frequency capping and fraud prevention.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device).

More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

The full version shows no ads, and the game does not request ads once the full version is unlocked.

3. Purchases

The full version is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play. Payment details are handled only by Google; the game receives only whether the purchase was made, to unlock the full version. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

4. Children

Join the Points! is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact me and I will help have it removed.

5. Your rights

Because I do not hold any personal information about you, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete. Data on your device can be removed as described in section 1. For data processed by Google, use the tools in your Google account and Android settings, or contact Google. For any question, write to msalvidecuenta@gmail.com.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
